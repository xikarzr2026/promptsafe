Privacy statement — no database, nothing seen, nothing saved.

PromptSafe operates entirely within your browser. There is no server-side component, no database, no user accounts, no cookies and no analytics. All text you enter is processed locally in this tab by client-side JavaScript; no portion of it is transmitted, logged, persisted or stored on any device or server. Because no backend exists, there is no copy of your data to retain, and closing this tab discards it permanently. This claim is independently verifiable: open your browser's developer tools (F12), select the Network tab, and confirm that no requests are issued while text is being processed.

          ▶ What PromptSafe does not do
        
        
    
          
  • It does not send your text, or a hash of it, anywhere — there is no fetch(), XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, sendBeacon or image pixel in the logic.
    • 
          
  • It does not write to localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB or cookies. Nothing is saved, anywhere.
    • 
          
  • It does not log your input to the console.
    • 
          
  • It does not auto-load anything from a URL you paste.
    • 
          
  • It cannot guarantee complete detection — regex is a net, not a proof. Always eyeball the output before pasting.
